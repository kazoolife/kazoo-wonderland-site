The Temple City Kazoo Orchestra

2026.09.06

FROM THE ARCHIVE

KAZOOMUSIC.NET introduced the Temple City Kazoo Orchestra in 2016 and again in 2020 — an ensemble that plays rock and disco classics on kazoos.

The 1978 EP “Some Kazoos”

Confirmable disc information shows that a 7-inch EP, Some Kazoos, was released by Rhino Records in 1978. Its four tracks are “2001 Sprach Kazoostra,” “Stayin' Alive,” “Miss You,” and “Whole Lotta Love.”

Rather than a large symphony orchestra, its charm is the boldness of replacing well-known songs with the sound of the kazoo. It shows that the kazoo is an instrument that creates laughter — and, at the same time, an instrument for testing ideas in arrangement and performance.
