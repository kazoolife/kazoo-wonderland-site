A Kazoo That Flies? The Airplane Kazoo

2026.09.06
Research in progressKAZOO WONDERLAND editorial status

FROM THE 2017 ARCHIVE

“A kazoo in the shape of an airplane. It has a classical charm, like a tin toy — quite lovely.”

The 2017 article introduced it as a product of a kazoo company in New York, and published two photographs.

Re-examined in 2026

We are re-checking the maker's name, the period of manufacture, how it was sold, and whether examples survive today. Links to the maker's site that can be traced from the original article will be updated once their current status is confirmed.

Its value as a collectible

Kazoos come in many designs — not only submarines, but airplanes, trumpets, and more. The Airplane Kazoo is a document that shows the kazoo not only as a sound-making tool, but also as a piece of design and toy history.
