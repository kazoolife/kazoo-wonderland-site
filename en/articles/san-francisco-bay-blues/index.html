“San Francisco Bay Blues” and the Kazoo

2026.09.06
Research in progressKAZOO WONDERLAND editorial status

FROM THE 2016 ARCHIVE

In 2016, KAZOOMUSIC.NET introduced “San Francisco Bay Blues” as a song in which the kazoo makes a memorable appearance, and pointed readers to a performance video by Peter, Paul and Mary.

Jesse Fuller and the kazoo

The song's writer, Jesse Fuller, was known as a one-man band who played guitar, singing, harmonica, kazoo, footdella, and cymbals all by himself. According to Oakland Public Library materials, the first recording of this song was in 1954.

However, we do not state that “the 1954 recording contains a kazoo solo.” In another performance record published by Smithsonian Folkways, the kazoo is confirmed among the instruments Fuller plays. It matters to record the instrumentation of each recording separately.

Revision history

  • 2016-11-07: First published on KAZOOMUSIC.NET
  • 2026-09-08: Published with the year of the first recording and the performance records that include a kazoo kept separate
