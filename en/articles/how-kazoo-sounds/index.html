KAZOO SOUND

Your Voice Becomes the Instrument — Why Does a Kazoo Sound?

2026.09.06
VerifiedKAZOO WONDERLAND editorial status

You do not blow it — you put your voice into it

Simply blowing into a kazoo does not produce its real sound. When you hum with your voice, the vibration travels through the air inside the body to a thin membrane, and the membrane vibrates.

Your voice creates the pitch

The kazoo itself does not create a scale; the pitch of the player's voice becomes the melody. That is why any melody you can sing can be tried right away, even with no experience on an instrument.

The membrane changes the tone

The membrane's vibration adds a distinctive buzzing component to the voice. The material, the condition of the membrane, the shape of the body, and how you use your voice all change how it sounds.
