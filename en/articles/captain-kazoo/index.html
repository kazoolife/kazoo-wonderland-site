THE KAZOO HISTORY

Captain Kazoo and the Vanished Kazoo Museum

2026.09.06
Research in progressKAZOO WONDERLAND editorial status

FROM THE 2016 ARCHIVE

The original KAZOOMUSIC.NET introduced a collection said to include more than 1,000 kazoos, and the online “Captain Kazooz Kazoo Museum.” In 2017 it added the news of his death and the disappearance of the site.

Who was Captain Kazoo?

The man who called himself Captain Kazoo in video archives and elsewhere was Howard “Captain Kazoo” Capp. He worked as a street musician, performance artist, and video maker, and presented his own web museum and label. To avoid confusion with anyone else of a similar name, this article uses only the forms of his name that can be confirmed.

How do we record a “vanished museum”?

The original site can no longer be viewed as it was, and claims such as the size of the collection or “the world's largest” — his own descriptions — need to be checked against archived pages and physical materials. This article therefore starts from the videos and the original article whose existence can be confirmed, and does not state unverified numbers or superlatives as present-day facts.
