KAZOOKA — The Adventure of the Electric Kazoo

2026.09.06
Research in progressKAZOO WONDERLAND editorial status

FROM THE 2016 ARCHIVE

The original article introduced the KAZOOKA as “the most famous of the original electric kazoos,” and recorded that Captain Kazoo also featured the product. Even at that time, it noted that it “no longer appears to be on sale.”

Re-examined in 2026

We are re-checking the maker, the release period, the construction, how it worked electrically, and when sales ended, against documentary sources. The phrase “the most famous” in the original article is kept as a record of its time, and is not stated as a present-day fact.

Plugging a kazoo in

The idea of connecting the kazoo's voice and membrane vibration to microphones, pickups, and effects continues today in the KAZOO shop and in performance experiments. The KAZOOKA is an important candidate document for tracing that lineage.
