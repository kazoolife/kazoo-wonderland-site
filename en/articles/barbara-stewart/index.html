THE KAZOO HISTORY

Barbara Stewart, Queen of the Kazoo

2026.09.06
Research in progressKAZOO WONDERLAND editorial status

FROM THE 2016 ARCHIVE

In October 2016, KAZOOMUSIC.NET introduced Barbara Stewart as a professional kazoo player and the author of a book on the kazoo, and published an interview video with her.

Musician, author, and kazoo evangelist

Barbara Dean Stewart (1941–2011) worked as a composer and musician, and was also known for performing, researching, and promoting the kazoo. She wrote The Complete How To Kazoo and founded the group Kazoophony.

Sources confirm her career as a flutist with the Rochester Philharmonic Orchestra and her work with Kazoophony as separate things. There was no ensemble by the name of “Rochester Philharmonic Kazoo Ensemble.”

Her playing, preserved on video

In a 2006 interview video, she treats the kazoo not as a joke prop but as an instrument capable of expression. Her work carrying this small instrument onto the stage and into education is itself an important record of kazoo culture.
