THE KAZOO HISTORY

Seven Things We Want to Verify for THE KAZOO HISTORY

2026.09.03

Collecting interesting stories is not enough to make a definitive book. We check sources, and record what we do not know as unknown. These are the seven lines of research for doing that.

Under reviewInformation, materials, and testimony welcome
  1. The prehistory of the “invention”Mirlitons before the kazoo, and other devices that sound a membrane with the voice.
  2. The name and the patentsStarting from Frost's 1883 patent, tracing how the name “kazoo” and its shape changed.
  3. Legends vs. confirmable recordsOrganizing the tales around Alabama Vest and Thaddeus von Clegg together with the presence — or absence — of sources.
  4. Materials and manufactureWood, metal, resin, paper. How makers, factories, and membrane materials changed the sound.
  5. Recordings and the stageIn which eras, and in which works and settings, did players choose the kazoo?
  6. Kazoo culture in JapanPreserving imports, sales, performances, events, and personal memories in chronological order.
  7. The kazoo from here onThe new roles being born in communities, education, live performance, and digital expression.
About offering materials

If you have old catalogs, instruments, photographs, recordings, or memories of events, please let us know, together with the period and how you obtained them. Before anything is published, we will confirm the scope of use and how you wish to be credited.
