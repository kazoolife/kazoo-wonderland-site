THE KAZOO HISTORY

Why Can a Kazoo Turn Your Voice into Music?

2026.09.03

A kazoo is not an instrument that creates new pitches by itself. It receives your voice and adds a different expression to it through the vibration of a thin membrane.

VerifiedThe basic principle of how it sounds

The star is your voice, more than your breath

First, close your mouth and hum “mm—.” Keep humming and bring the kazoo to your lips: the changes in pressure from your voice reach the membrane, and a buzzing tone is born. When it does not sound, the shortcut is not to blow harder, but to voice a little more clearly.

The same kazoo sounds different in every person's hands

Pitch follows the note you sing, strength follows your volume and airflow, and rhythm follows the shape of your tongue and words. The kazoo does not hide the player's voice — it turns the character of that voice into an interesting tone. That is what makes it a kazoo.

30 SECOND TRY

Start with “mm—”

  1. Voice “mm—” without the instrument
  2. Without stopping your voice, bring the kazoo to your lips
  3. Move slowly between a high note and a low note

A mechanism that leads into history

The mechanism of vibrating a membrane with the voice is not confined to the kazoo alone. In THE KAZOO HISTORY we also examine its relationship with similar sounding devices such as the mirliton, keeping names, dates, and sources separate.
