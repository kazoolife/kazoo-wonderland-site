RESEARCHKARCHIVE

HOW WE BUILD THE BOOK

Verify, while publishing.

Rather than rushing a finished manuscript, we publish an article for each theme and update it as we check it against materials and testimony.

01

Gather materials

Books, patents, catalogs, instruments, recordings, photographs, and personal memories.

02

Verify in articles

We publish confirmed facts separately from theories still under research.

03

Connect voices

Working with KAZOOTO and IKPA, we gather information from Japan and abroad.

04

Weave it into a book

When the material has grown enough, it becomes the book THE KAZOO HISTORY.

EDITORIAL POLICY

Making the certainty of information visible.

Verified

Content confirmed by primary sources or several reliable sources.

Under review

Widely known claims whose sources and dates we are still checking.

Testimony & memory

The experience of players and makers, recorded with care, separately from the facts.
