What Is a KAZOO?

The kazoo is often treated as a kind of toy whistle, but it is a genuine instrument, classified as a membranophone. It produces sound from the vibration of a membrane stretched over an opening — a relative of the taiko and the drum, in the percussion family. Its prototype is said to have come from Africa, and the kazoo in its present form was born in America.
