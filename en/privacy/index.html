Privacy Policy

This page is an English translation of the Japanese privacy policy, provided for reference. This shop recognizes the importance of protecting customers' personal information. We comply with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (the “Personal Information Protection Act”) and handle and protect personal information appropriately in accordance with the following privacy policy (this “Privacy Policy”). 1. Definition of personal information In this Privacy Policy, “personal information” means personal information as defined in Article 2, Paragraph 1 of the Personal Information Protection Act: information relating to a living individual that can identify a specific individual by name, date of birth, or other descriptions contained in the information (including information that can easily be cross-referenced with other information to identify a specific individual), or information that contains an individual identification code. 2. Purposes of use of personal information This shop uses customers' personal information for the following purposes: (1) To provide this shop's services (2) To provide information about this shop's services and to respond to inquiries (3) To provide information about this shop's products and services (4) To respond to acts that violate this shop's terms, policies, etc. (the “Terms, etc.”) relating to this shop's services (5) To notify customers of changes to the Terms, etc. relating to this shop's services (6) To help improve this shop's services and develop new services (7) To create statistical data, processed into a form that cannot identify individuals, in connection with this shop's services (8) For other purposes incidental to the above purposes of use 3. Changes to the purposes of use This shop may change the purposes of use of personal information within a scope reasonably considered relevant, and will notify customers or publish the change when it does so. 4. Restrictions on the use of personal information This shop will not handle personal information beyond the scope necessary to achieve the purposes of use without the customer's consent, except where permitted by the Personal Information Protection Act or other laws. However, this does not apply in the following cases: (1) Where based on laws and regulations (2) Where necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property, and it is difficult to obtain the customer's consent (3) Where particularly necessary to improve public health or promote the sound development of children, and it is difficult to obtain the customer's consent (4) Where cooperation is necessary for a national institution, a local government, or a person entrusted by them to carry out affairs prescribed by laws and regulations, and obtaining the customer's consent may hinder the execution of those affairs 5. Proper acquisition of personal information This shop acquires personal information properly, and does not acquire it by deception or other improper means. 6. Security management of personal information This shop provides necessary and appropriate supervision of its employees so that personal information is managed securely against risks such as loss, destruction, falsification, and leakage. When entrusting the handling of personal information, in whole or in part, this shop provides necessary and appropriate supervision so that the entrusted party manages the information securely. 7. Provision to third parties Except where disclosure is permitted under the Personal Information Protection Act or other laws, this shop does not provide personal information to third parties without the customer's prior consent. However, the following cases do not constitute provision to a third party as defined above: (1) Where this shop provides personal information in connection with entrusting the handling of personal information, in whole or in part, within the scope necessary to achieve the purposes of use (2) Where personal information is provided in connection with the succession of a business due to a merger or other reasons (3) Where personal information is used jointly in accordance with the provisions of the Personal Information Protection Act 8. Disclosure of personal information When a customer requests disclosure of personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act, this shop will, after confirming that the request is from the customer themselves, disclose it without delay (or notify the customer if the personal information does not exist). However, this does not apply where this shop is not obliged to disclose under the Personal Information Protection Act or other laws. 9. Correction, etc. of personal information When a customer requests correction, addition, or deletion of personal information (collectively, “correction, etc.”) in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act on the grounds that the information is not true, this shop will, after confirming that the request is from the customer themselves, conduct the necessary investigation without delay within the scope necessary to achieve the purposes of use, make the correction, etc. based on the results, and notify the customer (or notify the customer if it decides not to make the correction, etc.). However, this does not apply where this shop is not obliged to make corrections, etc. under the Personal Information Protection Act or other laws. 10. Suspension of use, etc. of personal information When a customer requests suspension of use or deletion of their personal information (collectively, “suspension of use, etc.”) in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act on the grounds that it is handled beyond the scope of the purposes of use announced in advance, or that it was acquired by deception or other improper means, and the request is found to have grounds, this shop will, after confirming that the request is from the customer themselves, suspend the use, etc. of the personal information without delay and notify the customer. However, this does not apply where this shop is not obliged to suspend use, etc. under the Personal Information Protection Act or other laws. 11. Use of cookies and other technologies This shop's services may use cookies and similar technologies. These technologies help this shop understand how its services are used and contribute to improving the services. Users who wish to disable cookies can do so by changing their web browser settings. However, disabling cookies may prevent the use of some functions of this shop's services. 12. Contact For requests regarding disclosure, etc., and for opinions, questions, complaints, and other inquiries regarding the handling of personal information, please contact us at the contact details shown in this shop's “Notation based on the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions,” or through the inquiry form on the shop's pages. 13. Continuous improvement This shop reviews the operation of its handling of personal information from time to time, strives for continuous improvement, and may change this Privacy Policy as necessary.
